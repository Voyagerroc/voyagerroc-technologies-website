## Yayıma hazır İngilizce PayRadar taslağı

Tracking a recurring payment starts with a few practical details: the amount, currency, renewal date and repeat interval.

In PayRadar, our own product project at Voyagerroc Technologies, we bring those details into one view. The recorded web demo shows adding a sample payment, exploring the Orbit 3D view and reopening the app after the network is disconnected.

The demo uses sample records. It does not test notification delivery on a physical phone.

For a business workflow, we would start with the same questions: who creates the record, what needs to happen next, and how will we check that the flow works?

## İkinci İngilizce içerik taslağı

A useful first software pilot needs a clear finish line.

Before building, we define:
• the person using it;
• the task they need to complete;
• the records and systems involved;
• the example that will demonstrate whether it works.

At Voyagerroc Technologies, this is how we propose scoping an initial web or PWA workflow. Features such as payments, notifications and third-party integrations need their own acceptance criteria.
