Recurring payments need a clear record: the amount, currency, renewal date and repeat interval.

PayRadar, our own product project at Voyagerroc Technologies, brings those details into one view.

Our recorded web demo shows three concrete steps:
- Adding a sample payment.
- Exploring the Orbit 3D view.
- Reopening the app after disconnecting the network.

The recording uses sample data. Notification delivery on a physical phone requires a separate test.

For a business workflow, we start with the same practical questions: who creates the record, what happens next, and what example will show that the flow works?
