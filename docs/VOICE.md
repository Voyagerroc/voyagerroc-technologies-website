# Voyagerroc editorial direction

The website uses direct product language and current AI engineering terminology. A reader should understand what is being developed, how AI is used and which claims can be checked in the portfolio.

For clients, explain the workflow and output in ordinary language. For developers and readers who follow AI engineering, name the relevant approach precisely. Put a short explanation beside a technical term. Avoid indirect questions and abstract slogans as section headlines.

## Writing rules

- Use concrete nouns and verbs: payment renewal, work sample, source record, approval, delivery.
- Explain the use before the technology. AI use is part of the approach; it is not evidence of quality by itself.
- Use current terms where supported: AI-assisted development, agentic workflows, context engineering, hybrid retrieval and human-in-the-loop. Do not label every application AI-native or call ordinary search RAG without evidence.
- Describe evals as a project-planning standard unless a specific implemented evaluation suite has been verified. Do not imply measured reliability from a general intention to test.
- Anchor capability descriptions in named portfolio products. Keep product stages and unverified scope visible.
- Describe an intended working standard as an approach. Do not invent customers, completed handovers, certifications, staffing, operating history or measurable results.
- Keep prospective commercial collaboration distinct from an already operating registered agency. Legal identity and obligations belong in the appropriate notices.
- Write English for an English-speaking reader. Preserve the meaning and factual limits without translating Turkish slogans word for word.
- Avoid generic promises such as powerful solutions, cutting-edge technology, seamless experience, global value and a culture of innovation.
- Do not disparage vibe coding, other developers or AI-assisted production. Show what Voyagerroc pays attention to through the work itself.

## Examples in the current copy

- Opening: “Uygulamalar. SaaS. Agentic iş akışları.”
- Context engineering: preparing instructions, data and evidence; connected to Prompt Operating System.
- Hybrid retrieval: keyword and vector search; connected to Pharos.
- Human-in-the-loop: approval-gated actions; connected to Bungalow OS.
- Contact: “Ürünü ve kapsamı konuşalım.”

SaaS-oriented development does not establish a launched multi-tenant subscription service. “AI” refers to artificial intelligence; no achieved superintelligence, foundation-model training or autonomous operation without limits is claimed. The collective brand voice does not establish a particular team size or corporate registration.
